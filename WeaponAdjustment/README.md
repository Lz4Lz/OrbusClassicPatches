## Weapons Adjustment

In the **Mods** tab next to the Graphics tab in settings, you have the option to adjust your weapon or reset current adjustment to defaults (which sets "Enabled" to false for that class in the config).
Adjusting the weapon works like Reborn, where you get the weapons model in front of you to adjust relative to your hand. Gripping the weapon and pressing Trigger saves the new weapon-pose.

If you want to transfer your wand adjustment from the old **WandAdjustment** mod, you can still edit the **BepInEx** config and set the values for the wand or any class manually.

You can also change Lend A Hand direction in the **BepInEx config** under the **Runemage** section.
